FocusBloom

Privacy Policy

Effective July 10, 2026

FocusBloom is a local-first productivity application. No account is required. Tasks, habits, completion history, focus sessions, theme preferences, and onboarding status are stored on the user's device. FocusBloom does not operate a publisher-owned server for this productivity data.

Advertising and consent

FocusBloom uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertising and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to collect and manage privacy choices where required. Depending on the user's region and choices, Google and its partners may process device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, diagnostics, and approximate location inferred from an IP address. Advertising is requested only after the consent SDK reports that ads may be requested.

Permissions

The app requests internet and network-state access for advertising, consent messages, and related network operations. It does not request contacts, precise location, microphone, camera, storage, SMS, call logs, accessibility, or health permissions.

Device backup

Android may include local preferences in device backup or device-to-device transfer when the user enables those system features.

Children

FocusBloom is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed to children under 13.

Deletion

Users can delete tasks and habits inside the app. Uninstalling FocusBloom removes locally stored app data, subject to any device backup retained under platform settings.
