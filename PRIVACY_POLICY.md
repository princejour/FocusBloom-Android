# FocusBloom Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 10, 2026

FocusBloom is designed as a local-first productivity application. No account is required. Tasks, habits, completion history, focus sessions, theme preferences, and onboarding status are stored on the user's device. FocusBloom does not operate a publisher-owned server for this productivity data.

## Advertising and consent

FocusBloom uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertising and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to collect and manage privacy choices where required. Depending on the user's region and choices, Google and its partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, diagnostics, and approximate location inferred from an IP address. Google is an independent service provider with its own privacy terms and controls.

Advertising is requested only after the UMP SDK reports that ads may be requested. Where required, users can reopen privacy choices from FocusBloom settings.

## Permissions

FocusBloom requests internet and network-state access only for advertising, consent messages, and related network operations. It does not request contacts, precise location, microphone, camera, storage, SMS, call logs, accessibility, or health permissions.

## Device backup

The Android operating system may include FocusBloom's local preferences in device backup or device-to-device transfer when the user enables those system features. These transfers are controlled by the device platform and the user's settings.

## Children

FocusBloom is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed to children under 13. The publisher must configure the app's store audience and advertising settings consistently with the final distribution plan.

## Data deletion

Users can delete individual tasks and habits inside the app. Uninstalling FocusBloom removes locally stored app data, subject to any device backup retained under the user's platform settings.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will identify the latest version.
